# M1 Review — Size Spike

## Scope

Curated and full-ABI builds, measured size, deterministic output, export reachability, pin integrity, and build engineering.

## Evidence

- CI run 33767637278 passed.
- Curated wasm: approximately 406 KB raw and 105 KB gzip.
- Full ABI: approximately 2.59 MB raw and 855 KB gzip.
- Two clean curated builds were byte-identical.
- Functional encoding and RGBA checks passed.

## Review findings

- Archive content verification was required for the toolchain itself, not only source tarballs.
- Dependency markers and cache keys needed complete pin, toolchain, and flag inputs.
- Export assertions needed to account for optimization, inlining, minification, and optional-codec stubs.
- Native-oracle work belonged with its first consumer in M4.
- The curated/full-ABI size decision was robust even though their initial optimization levels differed.

## Disposition

All findings were either implemented in M2/M4 or recorded with an explicit milestone owner. Toolchain archive verification, exact cache invalidation, same-optimization comparison, and independent reproducibility later closed the material items.

## Decision

Gate passed with user confirmation. The curated artifact became the default and the full ABI the escape hatch.
