# M5 Review — Worker Session and Bundler Matrix

## Scope

Concurrency, arena lifetime, close and terminate semantics, in-flight failures, worker asset resolution, and bundler output layouts.

## Confirmed findings

- Session termination needed to be a public API rather than an internal hook.
- The esbuild fixture could bundle successfully while leaving the worker unable to find wasm at runtime.
- Asynchronous methods needed consistent poisoning and rejection shapes.
- In-flight termination and intermediate-image cleanup required direct tests.

## Fix round

- Public "terminate()" was added and poisons the session.
- The worker entry has a safe default branch and single-shot initialization gate.
- The esbuild fixture copies wasm and asserts the final output layout.
- Worker tests cover close, terminate, in-flight rejection, transfer, and failed-run cleanup.
- All fixtures use pinned pnpm, including nested workspaces.

## Evidence

Local lightweight checks passed with 95 tests. CI runs 33942042722 and 33942410612 passed all worker and bundler checks.

## Decision

Pass. All blockers and warnings were closed.
